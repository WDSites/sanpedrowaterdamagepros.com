About San Pedro Water Damage Pros

San Pedro Water Damage Pros answers the 24/7 emergency line for water damage in San Pedro and nearby towns and explains scope and price before work starts.

What we do

We handle water damage emergencies and the work that follows: extraction, drying, cleanup, repair and mold remediation. The business address is 1437 S Pacific Ave in San Pedro, and the line is answered around the clock, every day of the year. Crews are licensed and insured restoration professionals.

This site does not list owner names, license numbers or customer reviews, and it does not claim awards or years in business. What we can honestly offer is a clear explanation of how the work is done, what it depends on and how to check any company you are thinking about hiring.

How a call works

  1. You call

    Describe where the water is, where it came from and how long it has been wet.

  2. Safety steps

    The person on the line tells you how to stop the source and stay safe while help is arranged.

  3. Assessment

    A crew looks at the damage with moisture meters and explains the scope and price before work starts.

  4. Work and records

    Drying, cleanup and repair run in stages, with photos and moisture readings kept for your records.

Standards we point to

ANSI/IICRC S500 is the published standard for professional water damage restoration, and ANSI/IICRC S520 is the published standard for mold remediation. We name them because they are the references the trade uses. You are welcome to ask any company, including us, whether its crews work to them.

We do not claim a company-wide certification, a warranty or a preferred-vendor relationship with any insurance carrier. Insurance decisions belong to your insurer, and nobody can promise that a claim will be approved.

What we do not do

  • We do not waterproof foundations. Restoration repairs damage that already happened.
  • We do not repair roofs or plumbing lines. We will tell you when a roofer or plumber has to fix the source first.
  • We do not give legal advice about leases, landlords or insurance disputes.
  • We do not promise arrival times. Same-day response is offered when available.
