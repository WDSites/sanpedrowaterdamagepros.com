Palisades runs from the bluffs toward the middle of San Pedro and mixes houses, condos and small apartment buildings. Here is what water damage restoration looks like in that mix.

Water damage in Palisades, from the bluff edge to 25th Street

Local real estate agents describe Palisades as the part of San Pedro that runs from the coast along Paseo Del Mar to about 25th Street, with Western Avenue and Gaffey Street as its other edges. San Pedro has no single official neighborhood map, so treat those lines as approximate and give your cross street when you call.

Agent market summaries list single family homes, condos and buildings with two to four units here. That mix matters, because a leak in a house stays in the house, while a leak in a small building often travels to a neighbor. For the work after drying, see water damage repair.

The 2011 landslide and what it says about the coast

The San Pedro Community Plan records that a landslide in 2011 destroyed a 600 foot section of Paseo Del Mar beside the White Point Nature Preserve. The plan describes the nearby Sunken City block as geologically unsafe, with public access prohibited.

For a homeowner this separates two problems. Water inside the house is a drying problem. Movement in the ground is a geotechnical and building safety problem. A crew can dry a wet floor, but it cannot tell you whether a slope is stable.

Cracks outside the house

A widening crack in a wall or patio, or a fence that has started to lean, should go to the Los Angeles Department of Building and Safety. Mention it on the call so the crew knows what else is happening, and keep the interior drying separate.

Rain, runoff and the rooms that take the first hit

Los Angeles gets about 14 inches of rain in a typical year, and most of it falls from January through March. In a wet week, roof edges, window frames and garage walls take the first hit. Storm water damage covers rain that gets inside, and roof leak water damage covers ceilings.

Safety steps while you wait

Find the valve

Close the fixture valve first. The main shutoff is usually where the water line enters the property, and LADWP can explain meter shutoff if the valve is stuck.

Keep clear of electricity

If water reaches outlets, appliances or the panel, keep out of that room and dial 911 or the power utility. Never reach for a switch while standing in water.

Record the damage

Photograph and film each room from the doorway before moving anything. Note the time you found the water.

Separate clean water from dirty

Anything that came up through a drain or toilet counts as contaminated. Keep family and pets out and leave the cleaning to professionals.

Cleanup, drying and repair in Palisades

The first visit measures how far the water traveled. In a small building that means checking the rooms above, below and beside the obvious damage. Extraction removes standing water. Air movers and dehumidifiers then dry floors, walls and ceilings. Structural drying explains how the readings decide when the equipment can leave.

When a leak crosses units, the plan should say which areas are in which unit. That helps owners, tenants and insurers see what was done where. The published ANSI/IICRC S500 standard is the usual reference for how this work is done.

Who pays depends on who owns what

Often the building owner's policy applies to the structure, and a tenant's policy applies to belongings, but wording varies a great deal. The cause also matters, since sudden failures and slow leaks are treated differently and flood usually needs separate coverage. Ask your insurer which applies. No approval is promised here.

Prevention that actually helps

  • Check under sinks and behind toilets for slow drips every few months.
  • Replace old braided washer hoses before they fail.
  • Keep gutters and downspouts clear and pointed away from walls.
  • Know where your shutoff valve is before you need it.

Questions about water damage in Palisades

Is emergency water damage service available in Palisades?

Yes, start by calling. Say what is wet, which unit it is in and whether water is still moving. Advice for the meantime comes on the call, and a specific arrival time is not something this page can promise.

Where can I check flood mapping for a Palisades address?

Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status not verified. Mapped zones can differ from one lot to the next, so an address lookup is the only reliable check.

Who handles a leak that comes from the unit above me?

Report it in writing to your property manager or association right away, then call so the water can be dried. Who pays depends on the cause, the lease and the policies involved.

Should I be worried about the landslide area when my floor is wet?

A wet floor is almost always a plumbing, roof or appliance problem. Ground movement shows up as cracks, tilting and shifting outside. If you see those, call the city building department.

Do you only dry things, or also repair them?

Both. Drying comes first, and repairs such as drywall, flooring and paint follow once readings show the structure is dry. See water damage repair.
