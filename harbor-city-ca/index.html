More than half of Harbor City homes are rented

Census figures show that about 54 percent of housing units in Harbor City are rented. That fact shapes almost every call, because water damage in a rented unit raises questions about who pays, who authorizes work and how the unit below is affected. Tenants should photograph the damage, tell the landlord or manager in writing and keep copies of every message.

Owners have their own decisions to make, such as whether to file an insurance claim or pay for repairs directly. In both cases the first steps do not change: stop the water, move belongings and get moisture readings so the scope is clear.

When water is spreading in Harbor City

Emergency calls here often start with a washing machine hose, a toilet supply line or a leak from the unit above. The emergency line is open 24/7. If water is coming through a ceiling, move belongings out of the drip path, put a bucket under the flow and keep away from light fixtures that are wet.

Harbor City covers about 2.6 square miles. It is bounded by Sepulveda Boulevard on the north, Western Avenue and the Harbor Freeway on the east, Anaheim Street and Palos Verdes Drive on the south and the Lomita and Torrance border on the west. It sits just north of San Pedro, so dispatch moves out of the home base.

Which water company serves your address?

Your water bill answers this in one glance. If it says LADWP, call LADWP about the meter or a street main. If it names California Water Service, call them. Either way, the leak inside your walls is a restoration job. A utility fixes its pipes up to the meter, and the building owner is responsible for everything after it.

Knowing this matters at 2 a.m. when water is spreading. Ask your landlord or look at the bill, and if you cannot find either, call our line and describe the situation. We will help you work out who is who.

Near the park, the hospital and the freeway

Ken Malloy Harbor Regional Park on South Normandie Avenue and Machado Lake, once known for very clean water, give the area its green edge. A medical center with a hospital sits on Pacific Coast Highway between Normandie and Vermont Avenues. Naming a landmark like these on a call helps dispatch place you quickly.

Harbor City was part of Rancho San Pedro, granted in 1784, and Los Angeles joined it to the harbor in 1906. The cross streets near Western Avenue and Anaheim Street are common reference points, so give the nearest one.

Before we arrive: if water is still spreading

Close the valve

Fixture valve first, then the main. In an apartment, ask the manager where the building shutoff is.

Unplug and stay dry

Do not touch wet outlets or appliances. Cut power from the panel only if you can stand on a dry floor.

Warn the unit below

Knock on the door below. Water moves down, and neighbors can save belongings if they know.

Protect and photograph

Lift valuables, then photograph the water and the source before cleanup.
