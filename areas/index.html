Home base: San Pedro, ZIP codes 90731 and 90732

San Pedro is a neighborhood of the city of Los Angeles that was once its own city until the 1909 consolidation. It sits on a peninsula, with the Pacific to the south and the harbor to the east. Water damage calls come from the bluffs near Point Fermin Park to the harbor streets near the Port of Los Angeles.

ZIP codes 90731 and 90732 are covered. Give the nearest cross street when you call, since Gaffey Street and the Vincent Thomas Bridge approach split the neighborhood into easy reference points.

Why each town has its own page

Water damage looks different from one place to the next. Wilmington is a mostly rented, industrial and port-adjacent neighborhood with a history of main breaks. Rancho Palos Verdes sits on moving ground at Portuguese Bend. Harbor City splits its water between two providers. Lomita runs its own water operations in a very small city, and Torrance is a large planned city with several water systems.

Each page lists the local water provider, the permit authority and the ZIP codes, along with an emergency checklist written for that town. If you need the service itself, the services page sends you to the right one.

Questions about coverage

How do I know which water provider to call about a meter?

Your water bill names the provider. Each town page explains the common providers in that area.
