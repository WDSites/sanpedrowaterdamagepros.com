How the services work together

Most jobs run in order. Standing water is extracted, the structure is dried, ruined materials are cleaned out and the rooms are rebuilt. A single job may touch three or four of these pages, and each stage gets its own scope so the price is easy to follow.

Industry practice for the first stages follows the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard, and mold work follows S520. Any company you hire should be willing to tell you whether its crews work to those standards. Scope and price are explained before work starts, whichever service you need.

Not sure which one applies? The homepage has a plain step-by-step overview, and the service area page lists the nearby towns we cover.

What these services do not cover

Restoration repairs and dries what water has already damaged. It is not waterproofing, roofing or plumbing repair, and a roofer or plumber has to fix the source of the water. We will tell you plainly if that is the real problem. Insurance coverage depends on your policy, and no company can promise that a claim will be approved.
