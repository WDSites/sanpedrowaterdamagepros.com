What to tell the line

A short, clear description helps dispatch send the right equipment. If you can answer these, you will save time on the call.

  • Your street address and the nearest cross street, especially near Gaffey Street or the bluffs.
  • Where the water came from and whether it has been stopped.
  • Which rooms are wet and roughly how deep the water is.
  • Whether the water looks clean, gray or like sewage.
  • Whether any water is touching electrical outlets, appliances or a panel.
  • Whether you own or rent, and whether a manager has been told.

Safety before the call

Electrical or gas risk comes first

If you smell gas, see sparks or water is touching a panel, leave the area and call 911 or the utility before you call us.
