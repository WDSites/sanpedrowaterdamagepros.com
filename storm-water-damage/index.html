What a storm does to a coastal house

Storm water damage is a different problem from a leaking pipe. Wind pushes rain sideways under doors and around window frames. Gutters overflow and send water down walls instead of away from the foundation. Runoff from a yard or driveway finds the lowest point, which is often a garage or a lower level room.

The community occupies a peninsula, with open ocean along its southern edge and the harbor to the east, so storms bring wind along with rain. Winter months carry most of the yearly total, and one event can deliver a big share of it.

A February 2024 atmospheric river is a fair yardstick. Climate researchers at UC San Diego tallied 8.51 inches for downtown Los Angeles across February 4 to 6, in a place that averages near 14 inches over a full year.

Storm safety before you touch anything

Treat standing water as unsafe until power is handled. If water touches outlets, a panel or low appliances, stay out and shut off power only from dry ground. Do not wade through water near fallen lines or into a flooded garage with an electric door opener running.

Watch for debris, nails and broken glass in wet areas. If water has risen high enough to touch cabinets or reach the walls, call the line before moving things. Moving the wrong item in the wrong order can spread damage across a room.

  1. Stop more water coming in

    Use towels, sandbags or plastic at doors and clear drains and gutters when it is safe.

  2. Remove standing water

    Pumps and extraction wands pull water off floors before it soaks into subfloor.

  3. Pull wet materials

    Soaked padding, baseboards and the bottom of drywall come out when they cannot dry in place.

  4. Dry the structure

    Air movers and dehumidifiers run while we log readings each day. The ANSI/IICRC S500 reference guides what counts as dry.

Who to call first: you, the landlord or the city

Ownership shapes the first call. Census data for Torrance put owners in just over half of occupied homes, so many storm losses there begin with a homeowner and an insurer. Harbor City leans the other way, with about 54 percent of housing units rented, so a tenant should write to the landlord and keep copies.

If water came from a public street or a failed main rather than from rain, mention that to everyone you call. For floods that rise from outside and cover a whole floor, see flood damage restoration. For water that entered through the roof, the roof leak page explains that path.

Ground and slope notes for the peninsula

Slopes on the Palos Verdes side add a separate worry. A governor's emergency proclamation in September 2024 covered land movement in Rancho Palos Verdes, where utilities were cut to hundreds of homes before most gas service came back in May 2025. That was ground movement and not storm damage, but it explains why drainage and shutoff points deserve attention near a slope.

If your home sits near a bluff or hillside, ask your insurer and your city what conditions apply before the rainy season starts.

Insurance, preparation and what to avoid

Rain damage and flood damage are often handled differently by insurers. Wind-driven rain through an opening may fall under a standard homeowner policy, while rising water from outside commonly needs separate flood coverage. Your insurer can tell you which applies to your policy, and photos and receipts back up the claim.

To prepare, clear gutters before January, keep downspouts pointing away from the house and move stored items off garage floors. Do not run a wet vacuum on a wet floor with a damaged cord, and do not close wet walls with paint or paneling. Neighbors in Wilmington and Torrance follow the same sequence of stop, remove, dry and rebuild.

Questions about storm water damage

Is emergency storm water cleanup available in San Pedro?

Yes. Any hour works. After a big storm many homes need help at once, so the crew sorts requests by what is wet and how fast it is spreading.

Is storm water damage the same as flood damage?

They overlap. Storm damage often comes from wind-driven rain, gutters and runoff, while flood damage usually means water rising from outside across a floor.

Can I use a wet vac myself?

For a small puddle, maybe. For a large area or near outlets, wait for a crew, since water and electricity are a real hazard.

How soon do I need to start drying?

Start right away. Materials left wet for a day or two can begin to grow mold.

Does homeowner insurance cover storm water?

Wind-driven rain through an opening may be covered, while rising floodwater usually needs separate flood insurance. Ask your insurer.
