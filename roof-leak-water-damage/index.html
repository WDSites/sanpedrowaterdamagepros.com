A roof leak is a plumbing problem with the pipe on the outside. Water enters at one spot, then travels along rafters, drywall and wiring before it finally appears as a stain several feet away. By the time you see brown rings on the ceiling, the attic insulation above has usually been soaking for a while.

This page covers the interior side of a roof leak: stopping the spread, drying the structure and getting the ceiling ready for repair. The roof itself belongs to a roofer, and we will say so plainly.

Why San Pedro roofs leak when they do

Rainfall in San Pedro averages about 14 inches a year, and January through March deliver most of it. A roof can sit dry for months and then face several storms back to back. Cracked sealant, lifted shingles and worn flashing around vents and chimneys usually show themselves during the first hard rain of the season.

The February 2024 atmospheric river shows how fast a quiet roof becomes a wet house. A University of California San Diego weather research center summary counted 8.51 inches downtown over three days, well over half of what a normal year brings.

Roof leak safety in the first hour

Move people and valuables out from under the wet area first. Put a bucket or tub under any drip, and lift furniture and electronics out of the path. If water is falling through a ceiling light fixture, leave the light off and turn that circuit off at the breaker box, but only from a spot where the floor is dry.

Never climb onto a wet roof during a storm. Call a roofer for a tarp or temporary cover, and call our line for the interior. A sagging ceiling can drop without warning, so keep children and pets out of the room until the drywall has been released safely.

  1. Protect the room

    Cover floors and furniture with plastic, move belongings out and place containers under drips.

  2. Find the wet area

    A moisture meter and thermal camera map the damp drywall, insulation and framing so nothing is missed.

  3. Open and release

    Crews cut small openings in a bulging ceiling to let trapped water out in a controlled way.

  4. Remove what cannot dry

    Wet insulation and soft drywall come out. Salvageable framing stays.

  5. Dry the cavity

    Fans and a dehumidifier stay on until moisture readings settle back to normal. The ANSI/IICRC S500 guide is the industry reference for that goal.

Where the roofer and the restoration crew meet

Order matters. A roofer has to stop the water at its source before drying can finish, or the next storm will undo the work. Once the roof is covered or repaired, we dry the house and hand it back ready for rebuild. The finish work, such as new drywall, texture and paint, is covered on the water damage repair page.

Insulation is a good example. Wet fiberglass loses most of its value and holds moisture against the ceiling, so it usually comes out and gets replaced after the drywall is dry. Skylights and recessed lights are other common leak paths, and both need a close look before a ceiling is closed.

Local notes for roof leaks near San Pedro

In the Los Angeles neighborhoods of San Pedro, Wilmington and Harbor City, rebuild work is overseen by the Los Angeles Department of Building and Safety. Ask the department whether your roof or ceiling work needs a permit before you hire anyone.

Rental homes make timing tricky. Roughly 60 percent of Wilmington homes are renter occupied by Census-era counts, so a leak often turns into a talk between tenant and landlord. Photograph the damage, notify the landlord in writing and keep copies, and we can work from the scope you share with them.

Insurance and roof leaks, in plain terms

Policies treat a sudden storm opening differently from a slow leak that went on for months. Sudden storm damage is often covered, while wear and neglected maintenance often is not. The roof and the interior can also fall under different parts of a policy.

Ask your insurer before you decide, and keep photos, the date you noticed the leak and any roofer invoices. Your adjuster may want to see the damaged materials, so do not throw out wet drywall until you have documented it.

Mistakes that make roof leaks worse

  • Painting over a stain before the ceiling and insulation above it are dry.
  • Walking in the attic on wet insulation, which hides nails and weak spots.
  • Poking a hole in a bulging ceiling without a plan for the water or the wiring.
  • Waiting for the next dry day to call, while the insulation keeps wicking water.

Questions about roof leak cleanup

Is emergency roof leak cleanup available in San Pedro?

Yes. Phone at any hour, say where the drip is and whether the ceiling bulges, and we will talk you through protecting the room while a crew is arranged.

Do you fix the roof?

No. A roofer repairs or tarps the roof. We dry and clean up the inside so the repair is not wasted.

Can I leave wet insulation in the attic?

Usually not. Wet insulation holds moisture against the ceiling and can lead to mold. It is normally removed and replaced.

How long does drying take?

Many ordinary losses dry in three to five days, and heavier soaking can take longer. Moisture readings decide when equipment comes out.

Will my ceiling need to be replaced?

Soft or sagging drywall is replaced. Firm, dry drywall with only a stain can often be sealed and repainted.

Does insurance cover roof leaks?

Coverage turns on how the water got in and what your policy says. A sudden storm opening and years of wear are often handled differently, so put the question to your insurer.
