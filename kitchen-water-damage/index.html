Kitchen Water Damage Restoration in San Pedro, CA

A kitchen has more water sources than any other room, and its cabinets, flooring and countertops make drying harder. This page covers what gets wet and what can be saved.

Why kitchens get hit so often

The kitchen holds the sink, the dishwasher, the refrigerator water line and often a nearby laundry connection. A loose drain trap, a split supply line or a dishwasher that overflows can put water into a room full of materials that hate it. Cabinet boxes are usually particleboard, which swells when wet and does not return to shape.

Water also hides in a kitchen. It drips down inside the cabinet, runs under the toe kick and travels beneath the flooring, so the visible puddle is rarely the whole story. A cabinet back that feels soft is a sign the leak has been running a while.

Kitchen safety before the cleanup

  1. Find the shutoff

    The small valve under the sink closes the hot and cold lines. If water is coming from elsewhere, shut the main.

  2. Mind the outlets

    Kitchens have many outlets at counter height and under cabinets. If water is near one, do not touch it and call.

  3. Empty the cabinet

    Take dishes, cleaners and food out of the wet cabinet. Food and chemicals should not stay in a wet space.

  4. Wipe and ventilate

    Dry accessible surfaces with towels and open windows.

  5. Call the line

    Tell us what leaked, what flooring you have and whether cabinets are touching the floor.

Older homes and rented kitchens nearby

Torrance was incorporated on May 12, 1921, as a planned community, and the older homes in that part of the South Bay often keep original cabinets and plumbing runs. Original cabinets can be harder to match, so ask early whether a damaged box can be dried and repaired rather than replaced.

Lomita counts 8,593 housing units, and the Census shows renters in a little over half of those that are occupied. A leak under a tenant's sink is often the landlord's repair, so report it in writing the day you find it.

Ask the Los Angeles Department of Building and Safety whether a kitchen rebuild needs a permit before cabinets are removed.

Drying cabinets, floors and walls

Drying a kitchen means opening it up in a controlled way. Toe kicks come off, so air can reach the cavity. Small holes in the cabinet base let warm air in. Wet insulation or laminate flooring that has swollen is removed, and any hardwood nearby is handled as described on the hardwood floor page.

Air movers and a dehumidifier run for several days, with readings logged until dry. The ANSI/IICRC S500 reference guides how drying is measured and when it ends. If a long-running leak has caused growth, see mold remediation before any rebuilding.

Insurance fit

A sudden failure, such as a supply line that split or a dishwasher that overflowed, is often covered in part. Long-term seepage and wear are often excluded. Take photos of the cabinet interior before anything is removed, and keep a sample of damaged material if the adjuster asks.

Avoidable mistakes

  • Installing new flooring over a floor that has not been tested dry.
  • Painting a swollen cabinet base instead of replacing it.
  • Leaving a cabinet door closed over a wet interior.
  • Reconnecting the appliance before the plumber checks the line.

Questions about kitchen water damage

Is it a kitchen emergency if the sink line keeps leaking?

If water will not stop or is spreading under flooring, yes, and the line takes calls around the clock. A stain from an older leak is better scheduled so the crew can bring the right tools.

Can swollen cabinets be saved?

Solid wood boxes often can. Particleboard that has swollen usually has to be replaced.

How long does a kitchen take to dry?

Drying commonly takes several days, and heavy soaking can take longer. Readings tell the crew when the structure is dry.

Do I have to remove the countertop?

Not always. The crew first tries to dry from below and the sides. Stone or tile that hides a wet substrate may need to come off.

Who handles the plumbing repair?

A plumber does. The restoration crew dries the room and coordinates timing around the repair.
