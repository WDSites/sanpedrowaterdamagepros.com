Privacy Policy

This site is call-only. It has no forms and does not ask you to type personal information into a page.

☎ Call (424) 550-5662See our services

What this policy covers

This policy explains what information may be collected when you visit sanpedrowaterdamagepros.com or call the phone number listed on it. The site is operated for San Pedro Water Damage Pros, at 1437 S Pacific Ave, San Pedro, CA 90731.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

Information you give us by phone

When you call, you may share your name, address, a description of the damage and details about your home or insurance. We use that information to understand your situation, route your request, explain scope and price and follow up on your call.

You choose what to tell us. If you prefer not to give a detail on the call, say so, and the person on the line will explain how it affects what can be arranged.

Information collected by the website

Like most websites, this site may receive basic technical information from your browser, such as your device type, pages viewed and approximate location. Our host and any analytics tools used may log this data. The site does not contain forms, accounts or checkout pages.

Phone numbers on the site open your phone's dialer. Calls are handled by a telephone system that may record call details, such as the time and duration.

How information is used and shared

Information from calls is used to provide the service you asked for, to improve quality and to keep records. We share it with crews and service providers only as needed to carry out your request, and as required by law. We do not use this site to sell lists or to publish personal details.

You can ask what information we hold about your call, or ask us to correct it, by emailing contact@sanpedrowaterdamagepros.com.

Cookies and third-party content

The site loads fonts and an embedded map from other providers. Those providers may receive your IP address and set their own cookies under their own policies. You can control cookies in your browser settings.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will post the new version here. Questions can be sent to contact@sanpedrowaterdamagepros.com. For terms of use, see the terms page, and for how to reach the line, see the contact page.

Our coverage area

San Pedro is the home base. Crews also work in Wilmington, Rancho Palos Verdes, Harbor City, Lomita and Torrance.
